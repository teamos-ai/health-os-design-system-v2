## Copy mechanics in the system

- **Australian English**, following the Macquarie Dictionary: colour, organise, centre, enrolment, program.
- **Sentence case** for headings, buttons, navigation, badges and labels. Uppercase only through `text-label uppercase` for eyebrows and table headers, never typed in capitals.
- **No em dashes.** Use a full stop, a comma or a colon. A middle dot (·) separates metadata.
- **No exclamation marks** in interface or marketing copy.
- **Dates** read 13 September 2026. **Prices** carry their currency: $297 AUD / month.
- **Buttons** say what happens next in two to four words: "Book the walkthrough", "Start the check", "Save changes".
- **Labels** are short nouns: "Published · 6 min read", "Today", "Setup progress".
- **Errors** say what went wrong and what to do: "Add the full email address, including the domain."
- **Empty states** point to the next small action.

## Numbers

- Numbers in the reference site are sample data and are labelled that way. They never move into marketing.
- Marketing copy uses only numbers the database allows, with their source.
- **No published testimonial, client result, count or logo.** The database blocks all of them (C21): none has been measured and no client has given written permission. The components exist and are finished (the Testimonials section), and everything in them is sample copy carrying a Sample mark. To publish one, get the outcome measured and the person's permission in writing, replace the quote, the person and the portrait together, and turn the Sample mark off for that one alone.
- No countdown pressure and no before-and-after images.

## Audience and archetype

Both are open decisions. The database calls the customer an operator; earlier design-system copy said practitioner. Until Tumai decides, copy in this system speaks to "you" and refers to "clients", and no document assigns an archetype.

## Health and wellness

Health OS runs the business, not the wellness work. Copy never promises a health outcome, and the imagery never implies one. The database's `07-compliance-and-guardrails/health-claims-and-scope-of-practice.md` sets the boundary.
