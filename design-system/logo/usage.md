# Logo

The Health OS logo is the gradient OS mark: a rounded square filled with the signature gradient, with "OS" in white. It is used on its own. There is no approved wordmark lockup.

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `health-os-logo.png` | The shipped mark, 500 × 500 with a transparent background. Use this file |
| `health-os-logo.svg` | Not a true vector: it wraps PNG rasters. Do not use it where a vector is expected |
| `public/health-os-logo.png` | The same PNG, served by the site. `LogoMark` renders it |

A clean vector mark and the question of a wordmark are open decisions (see [REFERENCE.md](../REFERENCE.md#open-decisions)).

## Grounds

The mark is used exactly as supplied on:

- the light ground (white),
- the paper ground (warm ivory),
- carbon.

On a photo, place it on a paper or surface chip rather than directly on the image.

## Size and space

- Keep clear space of at least a quarter of the mark's width on every side.
- 16px is the smallest size in use (the favicon). Prefer 24px or more in layouts.
- In navigation the mark sits at 32px; in a footer or email, 32 to 40px.

## Keep it as supplied

- Do not recolour, reverse or re-angle the gradient.
- Do not stretch, crop, rotate or re-round the corners.
- Do not add a shadow, outline, glow or texture.
- Do not rebuild the "OS" letters in another typeface.
