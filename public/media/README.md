# Media

`overview.mp4` is the design-system video shown in the Video section, between the hero and Overview. To use a different file, change the `src` on the `VideoPlayer` in `src/showcase/sections/VideoSection.tsx`.

`cards/` holds the card images used by the card examples. Their tags and suggested uses live in `src/data/media.ts`.

Recommended video format: MP4 (H.264), 16:9, 1080p or smaller.
