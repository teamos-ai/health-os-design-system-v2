# Impeccable context: Health OS design system

Source: Tumai's answers in the Codex interview of 13 September 2026 (`health-os-ds-rebuild/DECISIONS.md`) and Tumai's section-by-section edits the same day. Tumai approves every design decision.

## Design Context

### Users

- **AI agents first.** Agents build Health OS pages, emails, posts, documents and product screens from this system, so every option needs a stated purpose, a "use it for", a "not for" and a correct example. Fewer, clearer choices beat more choices.
- **People second.** Tumai and the team browse the reference site to check, approve and pick parts.
- **End audience.** Owners of established wellness businesses and their clients. The audience noun is an open decision; the Health OS database calls them operators.

### Brand Personality

- Tumai's words: calm, minimal, open, free, relaxing, boho, technology first.
- Three words: calm, open, well-made.
- The feeling to leave: relief and confidence. Never hype, pressure or noise.
- Voice and claims come from the Health OS database (`teamos-ai/db-health-os`): direct, plain, unhurried, Australian English.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Themes:** light (white ground) and paper (warm ivory with a faint reading-paper grain). No dark theme.
- **Colour:** Sunlit Apricot, Expressive Rose and Neutral Lavender, each at full strength with two lighter shades and nothing darker, for a variation of calm. Text stays in warm ink neutrals. Colour is used in soft washes and small, deliberate moments. Apricot is the one interactive accent and never decoration: buttons, ticks, toggles, selected states and link underlines are apricot-200 (#F8C39C) with ink text. Focus rings are ink-900 so focus and selection never look alike. Nothing sits on a dark background.
- **Type:** Spline Sans headings; Anonymous Pro monospaced body and labels. The mono body is Tumai's chosen texture for "technology first" and stays.
- **Signature:** two-tone H1s with two or three photoreal picture tiles between the words; the apricot to rose to lavender gradient, used once per view; textured grounds on alternating sections; a thin ticker; a rounded light footer panel.
- **Motion:** Health OS's own, calm and flowing, on by default. BorderGlow, PointerSpotlight and GradientShimmer were removed at Tumai's request; Parallax stays experimental.
- **Anti-references:** hype SaaS landing pages, dark neon dashboards, glassmorphism, stat bands and testimonial walls, countdown pressure, generic stock-wellness clichés, cards that all look the same.

### Design Principles

1. **Less is more.** About three strong options per category. The approved exceptions are nine card types, the full functional widget library, the full image library and open-ended motion.
2. **Tokens or nothing.** Every value comes from `tokens.json`; the build fails on anything else.
3. **One moment per view.** One gradient, one primary action, one two-tone headline.
4. **Function before decoration.** Widgets take real data; images carry a suggested use; numbers are sample data unless the database allows them.
5. **Docs move with the design.** The catalogue and tokens change in the same pass as the components.

### Deliberate departures from Impeccable defaults

These are Tumai's decisions and are not audit failures:

- Monospaced body text (Anonymous Pro).
- The warm ivory paper theme.
- The video player's translucent, blurred carbon control bar over the video. Tumai chose this as the one video style; it is the only glass-like surface in the system.
- Marquee and ticker loops, on by default.
- Group eyebrow labels, kept to the first section of each group.
- The command hero's coded product preview, until a real screenshot replaces it (an open decision).
- The ink-400 rest of a two-tone headline: lighter than body text is allowed, but only at heading size.
- Photoreal object tiles inside H1s (Tumai's headline style).
